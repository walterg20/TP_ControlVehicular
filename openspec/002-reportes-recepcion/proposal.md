# Proposal: Spec 2 - Gestión Operativa y Reportes de Recepción

## Problem Statement
Currently, receptionists lack an efficient way to query the historical records of a vehicle or a comprehensive list of vehicles associated with a specific client. This creates delays during the vehicle intake process and limits customer service capabilities.

## Proposed Solution
Implement two operational reports for the reception flow:
1. **Historial de Vehículo (Vehicle History Report)**: To quickly retrieve past interventions, status changes, and general information about a vehicle.
2. **Vehículos por Cliente (Client Vehicles Report)**: To list all vehicles registered under a specific client.

## Business Value
- **Faster intake**: Receptionists can quickly verify past services and client history.
- **Better customer service**: Immediate access to data enables better communication with the customer.
